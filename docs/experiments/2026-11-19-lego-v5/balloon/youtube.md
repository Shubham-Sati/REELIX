# Title
Why a Sheep Flew Before Any Human (1783) #Shorts

# Description
In 1783, a king watched a sheep, a duck and a rooster fly over Versailles before any human went up. Here is how the hot air balloon got there, starting with a fire and a lot of smoke.

Joseph Montgolfier watched smoke rise from his fireplace and built a light silk box that floated to the ceiling. He and his brother Étienne then sent a huge balloon over their town of Annonay in June 1783. Nobody knew if people could breathe up high, so the animals went first. They flew about eight minutes and landed alive and well. Two months later, people flew.
