# set-builder memory
Lessons from earlier 3D videos. Add 1-3 short lessons after each video; keep this file under 60 lines (move a crowded topic into its own file and leave a pointer here).

- Kit test (2026-11-14): a 4 s shot with 2 figures, fire and smoke renders in about 8 s. With the camera at z 6-7.5 and fov 40, figures at x = +-1.4 sit at the frame edges in 9:16: keep characters within x = +-1.0 or move the camera back.
- tools/still.sh needs an ABSOLUTE output path (it changes folder before saving). Make a still for every custom shot, the hook included (v5 S1_0 had none).
- 9:16 at fov 42: visible width is about 0.38 x camera distance; a medium shot of one figure needs the camera about 4.5 m away.
- Reuse, don't rewrite: videos/2026-11-19-glass-from-sand/project/custom/_kit.tsx has AtHand/AtHead/handWorld (props glued to hands), Dressed (costume overlay on Toy), Spoon/Hammer (stick from hand to a target, `fit` length), Flames, PotOnFire, Furnace, TinBath, HouseFront.
- Long horizontal things (conveyor, float bath, rows) never fit 9:16 side-on: rotate the set group ~-0.75 rad so it runs back-left to front-right, and have the camera follow a point on the line.
- Colour washout: the kit Fire (emissive 1.4 + pointLight 6) and any emissive above ~0.7 turn orange to white/pale yellow. Use emissive 0.3-0.7 and lights of 2-5. Check the colour in the still, not the hex code.
- Kit quirks: Town centres its houses on p.x, so use p.x = 0. Animals in WickerBasket sink below the rim unless raised about 0.2. Sky shots need an extra ambient light or clouds turn muddy. Keep waving crowds in open space, not between houses (v5 S4_1).
- v7: props named in the narration (limestone) must be large (about 2.4x the first attempt) and visible for the whole word, and the drop/splash should land on the spoken word. Hook shots need a fast event in the first 0.4 s, not a slow fill.
- v8 rock-salt (no cast, pure diorama): a box basin with the front cut open (transparent water box, salt bands on the floor, tide-ring strips) reads clearly at camera 7-9 m, fov 40; reuse Basin/SaltRain/FillGauge/MineTunnel/SaltCrystal from videos/2026-11-19-rock-salt/project/custom/_kit.tsx. Top-down map sets (strait) need the camera ~10 m up or lumps/blocks fill the frame.
- v9 blue-marble (space + cabin): reuse Earth (lat/lon vertex-colour globe, Blue Marble view), Stars, Moon, CSM, DottedArc, Cabin, FilmCamera, PhotoPrint, Iris, Magnifier, BalanceScale, Astro (crew-stripe overlay) from videos/2026-11-19-blue-marble-mystery-photographer/project/custom/_kit.tsx.
- Framing traps (v9): a figure seen from behind hides what its hands hold, so turn it rotY ~pi+0.5. A wall that "falls away" must slide down out of frame; tipping it toward the camera filled the frame with grey. A medium close-up of a 1.12-tall toy needs the camera at least 2.7 m away (fov 40); 2.1 m gave a head-only shot.
- Batch stills from the repo root with a bash script (zsh does not word-split "name sec" pairs). Check colour contrast in the still: a white gauge fill on a white face, milky water hiding cubes, and overlapping transparent boxes all looked wrong; keep only the main body transparent, the rest opaque.
- v10 meteorite (living room + people): reuse Person (CAST + dress Skirt, pin curls, bun/glasses, Handbag), HeadMount, SleepLids, lying->sitting pivot (S2_2), Couch/Radio/Quilt/Ceiling-with-hole, House (clapboard, patch, snow), SeasonTree, Fireball/Trail/Shatter/Puff, GlassCase/Pedestal/Beam, GlowArc from videos/2026-11-19-meteorite-lego/project/custom/_kit.tsx. The kit's Box is not exported: add `export { Box, mat }`.
- Face overlays: AtHead ignores the Toy's idle roll (~0.03 off), so use HeadMount; closed lids must sit at z >= 0.132 with depth 0.66 or the pupils poke through. A Glow or emissive on a "black" rock turns it orange-brown: keep the rock plain (#1d1b1a, no glow) when it isn't in flight.
- Debris (Shatter) pieces must be short in depth (split long section slabs into 0.4 m segments) or flying planks fill the camera. A seated toy facing the camera reads as standing: turn it rotY ~0.45 and cover the lap. Pans/tilts should reach the spoken subject by the word, not at the end of the shot (no head behind the top label mid-tilt).
- v11 jolanda batch 2: reuse ShipFrame (overlays that heel with a Liner), DashedBox (dashed "missing part" outline), RowBoat, RopeLadder, ThumbsUp, SaloonSection (bow-on room cutaway on a hull section), Crate from videos/2026-11-20-principessa-jolanda-launch/project/custom/_kit.tsx. A Toy is ~1.7 units tall (spec height 0.98), so a liner next to people needs scale ~4.
- Ship lean next to a person: heel AWAY from the camera and yaw the ship ~pi+0.85 (three-quarter) so the tilted funnels/mast read; a rope ladder must lie on the sloping hull (shipPoint top/bottom), not hang vertically. Splash foam seen through a glass hull shows as a flat grey oval: drop it. In shell scripts, quote "$L/still-x.png" (the repo path has spaces).
- v10 fix lessons: open wide on a figure when a prop sits on a body part (bruise on thigh below the hem), then tilt later; two people in one shot need x within about +-1.0 or Ann gets cut by the frame edge (S3_1). Build hook and payoff shots with the cast (tug-of-war, hand-over) in the first pass, with gripping hands, so they are not left as cards.
- (Updated 21 Nov) Text now lives only in bands: labels in the top band (16:9 y < 165 px, 9:16 y 150-330), captions (off by default) in the bottom band. Keep the action in the middle band; the old mid-frame label/caption numbers no longer apply. Reuse MedMap (map relief with shrinking sea), BasinTank, DrillRig, MeasureRod, Halite, RockSalt (open), NaClLattice (build/split), Balance, Shaker/Heap, Person (glasses, hard hats, whiskers, vests) and Horse from videos/2026-11-20-how-rock-salt-is-formed-the-sea-that-dried-up-and-left-salt-up-to-3-km-thick/project/custom/_kit.tsx.
- Rock-salt v12 set pieces (domes, Gulf map, Great Salt Lake, Badwater, tank strata, Nile/Gibraltar cutaways): see rock-salt-v12.md (read when a set is a map, tank cutaway or salt flat)
- Cutaways: a drill pipe inside opaque layers vanishes; put it on the cut face (z = front + 0.04) with a dark borehole strip. A clear crystal on a grey studio needs a cyan tint (#8fcbe6, opacity 0.6) and glowing white edges. A floating shaker must pivot in the air, or tipping it sinks the cap into the plinth.
- Shots are split evenly over a line, so the storyboard's per-shot action can land on the wrong words (S2_2-S2_4): check captions and move the action to the shot where its word is spoken.
- v13 bus batch 3: "ground turns green" tiles must card-flip (scale.x = |cos pi k|, swap colour at k 0.5, lift ~0.25); rotating a 2-3 m tile about its edge stands it up and fills a close camera. A vehicle that drives in and stops for a push-in must face the camera's END position (S4_1 first ended on the bus's rear). Shots that continue the last one (S4_1 after S4_0) copy its rng seed and its end camera.
- Thin boxes (tiles under 0.05 tall) made with the kit Box cast/receive shadows and come out dark and blotchy: use a plain mesh with no shadows. A white "glowing" salt bed on a white studio is invisible: tint the glow warm (#ffe39a).
- Two-hand prop: place it at the midpoint of handWorld(R) and handWorld(L), turned along R-L, and put the tray at the grab pose's midpoint so the pickup doesn't jump. ToySun casts a grey shadow blob: keep it far behind the set.
- A still at the shot's middle can miss the action (wobble/colour change came later and a missing `salt={salt}` prop went unseen): also render one frame after the key word for every shot whose change happens late.

- Keep tall props out of the top band, where the labels sit. Kit ToySun casts a shadow blob on a near cliff: draw a local sun without castShadow. Shallow flood water over a flat needs opacity ~0.45 or the dissolving crust underneath can't be seen.- Vertex-colour stripes (salt bands) smear into blurry blobs unless the geometry has ~40 profile points per metre; keep wall-bump noise low-frequency or it turns into fish scales. Interiors (tunnels) wash out under the Stage lights: dim the colours (x0.7), ambient ~0.1.
- v16 farm-to-plate batch 2: still.sh/render.sh copy only model paths written as PLAIN strings; a template path (`models/.../${f}.glb`) is never copied and the model silently vanishes (S2_3 skyline). Reuse HandProp/handW/midHands (crouch-aware hand props), SeatedPerson (+handR), Stick (spoon/knife/ladle palm->target), CoinStack, ProducePile, ShopShelf, KitchenRoom/KitchenIsland/DiningTable and props grocery-shop, traffic-signal from videos/2026-11-21-from-farm-to-your-plate/project/custom/.
- Faces: kit "joy" above exprK ~0.6 tilts adult brows into a cross look; use joy 0.5 for adults, "laugh" for kids. Kit Smoke puffs grow to ~0.5 m (a grey cloud over the cook): draw pot steam as 5-8 small fading spheres. Long exponential pull-backs (plate -> whole map): move the look target by the distance fraction (d-d0)/(d1-d0), not by u, or mid-shot the camera stares at the floor.
- v15 one-bus-40-cars (9:16, model library): cyclists were Mini seated (torso only) on the library Bicycle, which also faces -z, so riders floated legless on backwards bikes. Use <Cyclist> (full rider with pedals, rides toward +z; rotY = travel direction, t = shot time, steer while turning) and pass flip for catalog lines marked flip.
- v17 lost-puppy (animal cast): reuse Pet (library animated animal from CAST: repaint by material name, normals smoothed with mergeVertices, collar+tag attached to the Neck bone, clip->clip2 crossfade via action.time + weight + mixer.update(0), bone offsets), Tower, CityStreet, Crossroads, EdgeOfTown, FarmYard (RidgeGround hill + road over the crest, yardY), hopPath (bouncing ball), ScentRibbon, SoundRings from videos/2026-11-21-the-lost-puppy-finds-its-way-home/project/custom/_kit.tsx. Quaternius animal Head bone: x = look up, z = turn toward +x, y = roll.
- 9:16 is narrow: horizontal half-angle is only ~12 deg at fov 40-44, so a subject must sit within about +-0.2 x its distance from the look line. First passes of S2_1/S3_0 lost the puppy off-frame; a 0.34 m dog next to traffic needs the camera ~4.5 m back.
- Isolated body parts can read rude: a stand-alone toy nose (tilted pill + two balls) looked phallic. Show a whole toy head with a big round nose instead.
