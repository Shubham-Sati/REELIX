# visual-artist: image checks (rejects, redo prompts, face checks)

- Common rejects so far: wrong era of clothing, wrong object (gift box for a silk box), character drift between shots, tall subject cut off at the top. Check these first.
- Smoke-idea-2: FLUX turned the "Montgolfier balloon" into a modern striped hot-air balloon with a basket in 4 of 5 shots, even with "no basket, no stripes" in the prompt. Also drifted to photoreal. Redo prompts should start with "Painterly 2D illustration, not a photograph" and say "perfectly round sphere, nothing hanging below".
- Beards and missing wigs creep in on the brothers (Joseph bearded, Etienne wig-less). Repeat "clean-shaven" and the wig wording. Only 4 redos are allowed, so prioritise the hero-object shots, then character drift.
- Also check small modern props and parallax mattes: video 2 had a wire hanger, lace-up shoes, a pocket watch and a tyre-like ring, and the cut-outs left a ghost edge (s4_2) and a bright jaw rim (s1_2).
- Smoke-idea-3: style split three ways (photoreal, 3D chibi, flat anime) even with the "not a photograph" opener. King Louis aged into a bearded old man when paired with another character. Glasses and a wristwatch crept in. Brothers were bearded again. Name the age and "no beard, no glasses" in every shot that has the king or the brothers.
- Smoke-idea-3: "exactly three animals" with a sheep gave two birds and no sheep. Describe each animal's position and a body trait (woolly, four legs). Backdrops for 3D overlay (custom engine) must be truly empty; the FLUX sky still got a small balloon.
- Smoke-idea-4: facematch distances were useless twice: Joseph's anchor was a side profile (scores inflated for every Joseph shot) and a bearded Joseph scored a "good" 0.708. At the anchor check, reject a pure-profile anchor and ask for three-quarter view; always confirm beard/age by eye, never by score alone.
- Smoke-idea-4: accepting an anchor with chin stubble and a 55-60 look (Joseph is 42) made every later Joseph read 55-65; Etienne's mid-20s anchor made him look young everywhere. Age and stubble on the anchor spread to all shots, so reject them at the anchor stage.
- Smoke-idea-4: a two-person shot (both brothers, s4_2) failed three times (glasses, beard, bare legs, wrong coat colour, photoreal, then Pixar-style child). After one failed redo of a two-shot, ask for two single-person shots instead.
