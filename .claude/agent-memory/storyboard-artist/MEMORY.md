# storyboard-artist memory
Lessons from earlier videos. Add 1-3 short, specific lessons after each video; keep this file under 60 lines (merge or drop old ones).

- FLUX image prompts (negatives, character drift, style, framing, hero objects): see flux-prompts.md (read before writing image prompts)
- Order shots by when their key word is spoken: in video 2 the laundry and Joseph shots were in script order but each ~2.5 s off the voice.
- Anchor 'shot' numbers are 0-based (file names s<scene>_<shot>); v4 used 1-based and an anchor was skipped.
- Don't use engine "parallax" for shots with a person: the background plate is not inpainted, so a ghost second head shows (v4: s1_1, s2_1, s3_3 all ghosted, P0). Use "image" for people.
- Hook (v4, P1): the kinetic title "A FAILED WAR PLAN" repeated the spoken words while captions showed them too, over a dark, blurred background. Give the opening kinetic text different words (date/place/number) and open on a clear, striking picture (e.g. the 3D hero shot) in the first second.
- v6: silk/cloth hero objects come out as rigid picture frames; prompt "soft billowing silk, gathered seams, no wooden frame, no picture frame" and keep ceiling beams visible if the line says it hits the ceiling. For Wan shots, write short motionPrompts that keep poses ("stays standing, no other people").
- v10 meteorite-lego (3D lego): kinetic cards draw no 3D set, so never use "kinetic" for a story beat with a cast member (the landlady tug in the hook, the $500 trade). Use "custom" with the people and put the words in a label. Card words must match the voice at that moment (a "1 PERSON. EVER." card played while "landlady" was spoken); save it for the line that says it. Every cast member in the cast list needs a shot, and the final beat should show the person named.
- v11 meteorite-ai: FLUX drew 2 meteorites, duplicate Julius, and horses instead of mules. Write "exactly one", "one man only" and the species ("long-eared mules") in prompts; and for Wan motionPrompts add "no faces on objects, no cut, camera stays wide" for rocks/radios.
- v12 venera-7 (toy3d): repeat of v10: the date card in the hook (3.8 s black) and the 475 °C payoff were both "kinetic", so the two biggest beats had no 3D set (P1 x2, score 7 -> 8 only after the fix job). In toy3d, make EVERY shot "custom" and put dates/numbers in a label; keep kinetic only for pure title cards with no story object.
- v13 principessa-jolanda (toy3d): scene 2 shots were cut in equal 4.155 s slices, so "rolls onto her side" played over an upright ship and "swings back" over the ship that kept rolling (P1 x2). Set shot starts from assets/lines.json word times, not equal splits; and plan a label for every term the voice names (LOW, HIGH, NO COAL, 0 DEAD) or the SFX pops play over nothing.
- v14 rock-salt (10 min, 106 shots): shots split evenly inside a line left 5 shots 4-5 s off their cue words (S2_4, S7_2, S7_5, S14_3) and a fix can't move them without deleting a neighbour. For long videos set each shot start from captions word times and re-split the scene; don't leave a cue earlier than the previous shot's start.
- v15 one-bus-can-replace-40-cars (toy3d, 60 s): equal-length shots again drifted off the numbers (bus lane 8,000 said 1.5 s before its shot; "1 BUS" cut in 2.4 s before "One bus held them all") = 2 P1 + 1 P2. Start every shot at the word that names its number/payoff, and give number shots just the length of that line.
- v16 from-farm-to-your-plate (toy3d, 56 s, 7 -> 8): third video in a row with equal-length shots missing key numbers (83% chart after the word, "surprise" coins 3 s late) and a kitchen shot framing half the cast. Cut each shot at the word that names its number/payoff, give every comparison shot per-item labels (which plate is California), and have the shot's own clock rise on that word.
- v17 lost-puppy (toy3d, 60 s, 7 -> 8): science beat shots ("compass check", "more direct", GPS map) started 1-3 s off their words again (4th video). Also the hook label named the wrong place ("THE FARM" over an edge-of-town set): label what the set shows. A comparison shot (100,000x) needs a full body per bar owner and bars that fit 9:16, or it reads as a bare head on a box.
- v19 village-to-city (toy3d, 5 min): shot "label" notes must name the beat at the shot START and the animator's inner cuts need their own note, or give one beat per shot. A scene label and a shot note must not repeat ("THE CITY" twice). Statistic figures ("two in three") come out as clones of the hero: say "anonymous passers-by, different clothes".
